Perform a static, elastic plastic analysis of a 2D beam undergoing uniform bending. We wish to obtain the residual stress after loading.  Create a deformable homogenous rectangular solid with a length of 100 mm and a height of 20 mm. Define the material with a Young’s Modulus of 200000 N/mm^2 and a Poisson’s ratio of 0.3. For the plastic material behavior hardening is isotropic.  The data field for yield stress and plastic strain have only 2 lines filled with data.  The first line in the data field is a yield stress of 300 N/mm^2 and a corresponding plastic strain of 0.0.  The second line has a yield stress of 310 N/mm^2 and a plastic strain of 0.01.  Define two Static, General steps. Apply all boundary conditions at a reference point at the centroid of each edge located at x = 0 mm and x = 100 mm.  Further, a kinematic constraint will be applied at each of the reference points for the moments.  The boundary condition at the two ends (x = 0, x = 100 mm) have displacement fixed in the U1 and U2 directions at the reference points located at the centroid of the edges. The loading for the first step at the x = 0 edge has a moment of -30000 N-mm acting at the reference point at the centroid of the edge.  At the x = 100 mm edge, the moment acting at the reference point at the centroid is 30000 N-mm.  Again, each of these moments act through separate reference points located at the centroid kinematically coupled to each edge.  The constrained degrees of freedom for each kinematic constraint is U1, U2, and UR3.  The second static step is the unloading step.  The loading for the second step is a moment of 0 N-mm applied at the same reference points as the first step.  The original applied load should all be unloaded.  All other boundary conditions remain the same as the first step.  For the mesh use CPS4I elements.  The mesh should have 40 elements across the span of the beam and 8 elements through the height for a total element count of 320. Extract the full body Displacement (U), Stress (S) fields, Strain (E), Plastic Strain (PE), and Equivalent Plastic Strain (PEEQ). Specifically, generate and display the full body displacement contours, full body stress contours, full body strain countours and full body Von Mises stress contours.
